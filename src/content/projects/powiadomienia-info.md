powiadomienia.info is a personal notification hub for households.

## How it works

- Enter an address for waste-collection schedules.
- Add the family's important dates.
- Connect a Telegram bot — privately or in a family group chat.
- Receive a short message exactly when it matters.

## Status

The product is in early access, with an email waitlist on the public site.

## Why it belongs here

Small consumer products like this one are where delivery habits get stress-tested end to end: onboarding, scheduling correctness, message timing, and the unglamorous work of keeping a service quietly reliable.
